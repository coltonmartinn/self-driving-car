How Driving School works

Practice the parts of driving that come up on every road test, as many times as you like.

🗺️

Real streets

Search any town, even your own, and drive its real road layout from OpenStreetMap.

🧑‍🏫

A coach in the passenger seat

Rolling stops, red lights, speeding, tailgating, missed signals, crosswalks: it catches them as they happen.

📋

Graded like a road test

Mistakes cost points, critical ones fail the drive, and every one comes with a tip.

🚙

Traffic that follows the rules

Other cars stop at lights and signs and keep their distance, so you learn to share the road.

🧭

Turn-by-turn directions

Follow the route like a GPS. Miss a turn and it reroutes, just like in a real car.

📱

Keyboard or touch

Drive with WASD or arrow keys, or with on-screen pedals on a phone or tablet.

For parents and teachers

Free tools to follow a new driver's progress, with no accounts and nothing to install.

📝

Permit test practice

40 questions on signs, signals, right-of-way and safe driving, each with an explanation.

 📋

Progress reports

Lessons passed, practice time and the mistakes that keep coming up. Print it, or send it as a link.

 👩‍🏫

Class view

Paste your students' report links to see the whole class, and the mistakes to teach next.

📱 Install it like an app on a phone, tablet or Chromebook, and it keeps working offline.

⚠️
Practice, not a substitute. This simulator helps you learn the rules, but it doesn't replace a licensed driving instructor, your state's driver's manual, or supervised hours behind the wheel.
