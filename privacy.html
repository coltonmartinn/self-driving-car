🔒 Privacy

Your data stays on your device

Self-Driving City has no accounts, no tracking, and no ads. Nothing a student does is sent to us.

What is stored, and where

Lesson results, drive history, quiz scores, the practice log, and the name typed on the certificate are saved in the browser's own storage on the device being used. They never leave that device unless the student chooses to share them. Clearing the browser's site data erases them.

Sharing a progress report

The Copy link button puts the student's report inside the link itself. Whoever the student sends it to can see that report. The link isn't stored on a server, and nobody else can find it. The class view keeps pasted links in the teacher's own browser only.

What does go over the internet

  • The app's files come from GitHub Pages, which hosts the site.
  • Fonts come from Google Fonts.
  • Searching for a town sends what you type to free map services built on OpenStreetMap data: Nominatim (or Photon by Komoot, as a backup) to find the place, then one of the public Overpass servers (overpass-api.de and its mirrors) to download its streets. The five built-in cities need no search.

These services see a request from your device, like any website does, and have their own privacy policies.

For schools

Because no student information is collected or stored by us, there are no student records to request, correct, or delete on our side. Teachers who collect report links decide how to keep them.

The source code is open (MIT license) on GitHub, so anyone can check these claims.
